# User Guide

Choose the task you want to complete in LabsManager. This guide covers first steps and the main ways to find people, projects, teams, organizations, financial information and dashboards.

- [Getting started](getting-started/index.md): open LabsManager and choose a path.
- [Understand the interface](getting-started/interface.md): move between areas and recognise available actions.
- [Find information](finding-information/search.md): search across the application or narrow a list.
- [View a person](people/view-person.md) or [view a project](projects/view-project.md): explore the records available to you.
- [View a fund](finance/view-a-fund.md), [read budgets and expenses](finance/budgets-and-expenses.md), or [use the Fund explorer](finance/fund-explorer.md): inspect financial information visible to you.
- [Use your dashboard](dashboards/use-your-dashboard.md) or [customize it](dashboards/customize-your-dashboard.md): follow the summaries available to your account.
- [Use the calendars](calendar/use-the-calendar.md): compare visible leave and project planning across records.
- [Find and view teams](teams/find-and-view-teams.md) or [manage team members](teams/manage-team-members.md): read team composition and update it when permitted.
- [Find and view organizations](organizations/find-and-view-organizations.md): locate institutions and funders, then read their visible relationships.
- [Generate reports and export lists](reports-and-exports/generate-reports.md): download a record report or filtered list when permitted.
- [Manage your settings](settings/manage-your-settings.md): update your account, interface, notifications and dashboard alert horizons.
- [Use the tools](tools/index.md): inspect transversal lists, the organization chart and imports available to your account.

Other workflows will be added in later sections.
