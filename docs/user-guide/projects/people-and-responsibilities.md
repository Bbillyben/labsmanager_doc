# People and responsibilities

Use the **Participants** section of a project's **Overview** to see who is involved. The section appears on the overview rather than as a separate project tab.

## Read participation

1. Open the project and stay on **Overview**. For this guide, open ORION — Optical Resilience.
2. Find **Participants** and locate the person you need.
3. Read the person's role, allocation, participation dates and active or inactive status together. Allocation is the share of their time recorded for this project; the dates show when that participation applies.

A person's name opens their record only when that record is available to you. The section can show a name without a link. Controls for adding or changing participants appear only when your account has the required permissions.
