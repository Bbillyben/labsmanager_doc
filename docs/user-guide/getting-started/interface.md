# Understand the interface

Use the main navigation to choose an area, a list to find an item, and its record to read related information. The same pattern appears across people and projects.

## Move between areas

Choose an area from the main navigation. A list shows the items available to you. Open a row to see its record. Use the record's own navigation to move between its sections without losing the current person or project.

## Read a record

Begin with the heading to confirm that you opened the right item. Then select the section that fits your task: for example, a person's projects or a project's participants. Related information may appear in several records. A link shown inside one record does not always grant access to the linked record on its own.

## Recognise available actions

Look for actions near a list, record heading or section. An action may be absent or unavailable when your account does not have permission for that item. If an action you need is missing, ask your local administrator about your access.
