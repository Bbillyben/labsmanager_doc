# Getting started

LabsManager helps laboratory users and project leaders find people, projects and the information connected to their work. Start at the home page, then open the area that matches your task.

## Open LabsManager

Sign in using the method provided by your organisation. Open the LabsManager application link supplied by your team. If you cannot sign in or an expected area is missing, contact your local administrator; access depends on your account.

## Start from the home page

Use the main navigation to reach people, projects and other areas available to you. The home page may also show recently visited items. Select a recent item to return to it, or use [search and filters](../finding-information/search.md) when you need to find something else.

## Choose a path

- To locate a person, use [search and filters](../finding-information/search.md), then [view their record](../people/view-person.md).
- To understand a project, [open its record](../projects/view-project.md).
- To learn where actions and related information appear, [understand the interface](interface.md).

The areas and actions you see depend on your permissions and the information available in your organisation.
