# Manage team members

You can change a team only when your account has the relevant permission. Open **Teams**, then select the team. If you cannot see the controls described below, ask a team administrator about your access.

## Create or edit a team

On the Teams list, choose **Add Team** when available. Enter a name and choose a leader if the form allows it. Some accounts have a fixed default leader instead. Save to create the team. On a team record, use **Edit** in **Information** to change the team details when that action is available.

## Add a member

In **Information**, choose **Add member**. Search for the employee, enter the membership start and end dates if needed, then **Save**. The person you choose must already have an employee record. The selector excludes the team leader and people already listed as members.

## Change or remove a member

Open the member row's action menu. **Edit** lets you change the employee or membership dates when permitted. **Delete** removes the membership after confirmation. These actions depend on permissions for that member, so two rows can offer different choices. When dates are entered, the end date must not precede the start date.

For the person's own information and work, open their [employee record](../people/view-person.md). Membership changes here do not replace the separate People workflow.
