# Find and view teams

Open **Teams** from the main navigation to find teams you are allowed to see. The entry may be absent if your account cannot view the team list. The list shows each team's name, leader and number of members; select a name to open its record.

## Find a team

Use the list filters to search by **Name**, **Leader**, or **Members**. You can sort by team name or the leader's surname and move through result pages. **Export** is available on the list. If you opened a team from a filtered list, use its back link to return to that list.

## Read the team record

The **Information** tab shows the team name and leader, then its members. Each member row shows an active or inactive status and its start and end dates when available. A leader or member's name opens their employee record only when you have access to that record. For more about an individual, see [View a person](../people/view-person.md).

![Quantum Materials Studio Information tab with leader and member rows](../../assets/screenshots/teams/information.png)

Other tabs show **Leaves** in a team calendar, **Projects** with their dates and status, **Budget** figures linked to projects, and **Notes**. The information shown depends on your access and the records available to your team. Select a project to continue in its record; see [View a project](../projects/view-project.md) for that workflow. The **Budget** tab is a reading view; see [Budgets and expenses](../finance/budgets-and-expenses.md) for project financial details.

If **Add Team**, **Edit**, or member actions appear, see [Manage team members](manage-team-members.md). Their availability depends on your permissions for the team.
